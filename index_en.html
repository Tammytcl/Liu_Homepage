Project Overview

Efficient Multimodal Generation

I study how feature dynamics across timesteps can reduce redundant computation in diffusion-based image and video generation and editing. Starting with predictive caching in TaylorSeer, my work explores forecast verification and adaptive computation in SpeCa, learnable stage-aware predictors in LESA, and frequency-aware caching in FreqCa to improve the quality–efficiency trade-off.

Interactive World Models

I contributed to Hunyuan-GameCraft-2, studying video generation controlled by natural-language instructions and player actions, with a focus on instruction following, action response, and long-horizon consistency. My work spans interactive video data cleaning and annotation, interaction-memory teacher-model SFT, few-step distillation, and evaluation, including Self-Forcing / DMD distillation and reinforcement learning for generation stability and visual quality.

LLM Post-training

I study on-policy distillation for knowledge transfer and multi-turn agents, including cross-tokenizer distillation with ESCD, heterogeneous MoE knowledge transfer, and MOPD. I also investigate multi-turn coding-agent tasks that combine reasoning, tool use, and environment feedback, focusing on training efficiency, supervision allocation, and task-solving capabilities with Terminal-Bench 2.0 and SWE-bench Verified.
